## Overview

*This software is neither affiliated with nor endorsed by either Control4 or
Nuheat (Chemelex).*

Control Nuheat Signature and Conductor floor-heating thermostats from Control4.
Each thermostat is a native Control4 thermostat with its floor temperature,
setpoint, mode and hold, so heated floors sit beside the rest of the home's
climate control in Navigator and in programming. The drivers use Nuheat's
official service, and the homeowner signs in on Nuheat's own page, so their
password is never entered in Control4.

## Features

- A native Control4 thermostat for each Nuheat thermostat
- Floor temperature, heat setpoint and Heat or Off mode, in Fahrenheit or
  Celsius
- Until Next and Permanent hold
- Sign-in on Nuheat's own page, from a short link sent to the homeowner or from
  the thermostat's Extras in the Control4 app
- Every thermostat on the account found, and added to its room in one step
- Floor temperature as a connection for other drivers
- Account Linked and Account Not Linked events for programming

## Included drivers

### Nuheat

The account driver. It links the homeowner's Nuheat account, finds every
thermostat on it, and adds a Nuheat Thermostat for each one with its Add
Thermostats action.

### Nuheat Thermostat

A native Control4 thermostat for one Nuheat thermostat, with floor temperature,
setpoint, mode and hold.

## System requirements

- Control4 OS 3.3+
- A Nuheat account with each thermostat set up in the Nuheat app
- Internet access from the Control4 controller

## Compatibility

Works with Nuheat Signature and Conductor thermostats. Nuheat's official API
does not offer schedule editing, so each thermostat's schedule is managed in the
Nuheat app and keeps running on the thermostat.
