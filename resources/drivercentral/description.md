## Overview

Control Nuheat Signature and Conductor floor-heating thermostats from Control4.
Each thermostat is a native Control4 thermostat with floor temperature,
setpoint, mode and hold, through the official Nuheat service. The homeowner
signs in on Nuheat's own page, so their password is never entered in Control4.

## Features

- A native Control4 thermostat for each Nuheat thermostat
- Floor temperature, heat setpoint and Heat or Off mode, in Fahrenheit or
  Celsius
- Until Next and Permanent hold
- Sign-in on Nuheat's own page, with a short link to send the homeowner
- Thermostats found on the account and added to their rooms in one step
- Floor temperature as a connection for other drivers
- Account Linked and Account Not Linked events for programming

## System requirements

- Control4 OS 3.3+
- A Nuheat account with a Signature or Conductor thermostat set up in the Nuheat
  app
- Internet access from the Control4 controller
